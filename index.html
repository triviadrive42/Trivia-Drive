HOW TO PLAY

Every game is NFL-style overtime. Everyone gets the same game each day. Win in as few questions as you can.

OVERTIME RULES

  • Coin toss: win it and choose to receive or defer.
  • Both teams get one possession, starting at their own 35.
  • After both possessions, whoever leads wins. Still tied? Sudden death: the next score wins.
  • If the team that kicked off scores a safety on the opening drive, it wins right away.

OFFENSE

  • EASY +5, MEDIUM +10, HARD +20 yards. 4 downs to gain 10.
  • Keep calling Easy or Medium and the defense catches on: those plays gain fewer yards each time. A HARD call resets it.
  • Inside the 20: Hard only, and a right answer is a touchdown.
  • Miss a HARD question: 1-in-4 chance it's intercepted.
  • Field goal (Medium) from the opponent's 30. Punt on 4th down.

DEFENSE

  • PREVENT (Easy): right holds them to 3 yards, wrong gives up 10.
  • BASE (Medium): right is no gain, wrong gives up 10.
  • BLITZ (Hard): right is a sack and a 1-in-3 takeaway chance, wrong gives up 20.
  • Answer a Hard question to block their field goals and stop their two-point tries.

BONUSES

  • ON FIRE: 3 right in a row makes HARD plays gain 25 and blitz takeaways 1-in-2.
  • Coach's challenge: once per game, throw the flag after a wrong answer for a new question.
  • Some days hide a trick play: one Hard question for 30 yards.

SCORING

  • Your result is the number of questions it took to win. Par is the estimated number for an average player that day.
  • Earn XP and trophies to climb from Rookie to Hall of Famer.

EVERY DAY IS DIFFERENT

  • A made-up opponent with its own style, themed days (Rules Tuesday, College Wednesday, Throwback Thursday, Legends Friday, Championship Saturday) and weather.
  • Sunday is a playoff game against an All-Pro CPU.
  • Every 7 days in a row earns a bye that protects your streak.
  • After you play, replay today's game as practice. It never counts.
